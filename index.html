King's Palace – Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 16, 2026

In short: King's Palace does not collect, store, or share any personal information. The game works fully offline and has no accounts, no ads, and no analytics.

Who we are

King's Palace (the "App", package name com.Kingstower.myapp) is a tower defense game for Android, made and published on Google Play by Jasandeep Singh ("we", "us").

Information we collect

None. The App does not ask for, collect, or transmit any personal or sensitive information, such as your name, email address, contacts, location, photos, device identifiers, or advertising ID. The App does not request internet access and cannot send data off your device.

Information stored on your device

To remember your progress, the App saves a small amount of game data only on your own device:

  • which levels you have completed and the stars you earned, and
  • your settings, such as sound volume, graphics quality, and camera zoom.

This data never leaves your device, is not shared with us or anyone else, and is deleted when you uninstall the App or clear its storage.

Third parties

The App contains no third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking services. Google Play, which distributes the App, may give us aggregated, anonymous statistics (for example, install counts) and crash reports. Google's handling of that information is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Children

King's Palace is made for players of all ages. Because the App collects no personal information, it does not collect personal information from children, including children under 13. The App follows the Google Play Families Policy.

Future changes

If we add new features that involve data, such as ads or rewards, we will update this policy and the App's Data safety information on Google Play before those features are released. The effective date above shows when this policy last changed.
